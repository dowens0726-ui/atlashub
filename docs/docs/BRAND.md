# Atlas Companion Brand Guide

## Mission

**Spend less time searching. More time playing.**

---

## Product Promise

**Find anything in GTA 6.**

---

## Vision

Build the fastest and most trusted companion platform for GTA 6 players.

---

## Brand Personality

Atlas is:

* Premium
* Confident
* Helpful
* Fast
* Trustworthy
* Modern
* Professional

Atlas is NOT:

* Clickbait
* Cluttered
* Loud
* Overcomplicated
* Generic

---

## Design Inspiration

### Rockstar Games

* Premium presentation
* Cinematic atmosphere
* Confident design

### Google

* Fast answers
* Simplicity
* Instant usefulness

Atlas combines both.

Beautiful enough to impress.

Useful enough to become essential.

---

## Design Language

Background:

* Near black

Accent:

* Emerald green

Secondary:

* Slate gray

Typography:

* Bold
* Clean
* Easy to scan

Animations:

* Fast
* Smooth
* Purposeful

---

## Core Principles

1. Every feature saves players time.

2. The answer is never more than two clicks away.

3. Information comes before decoration.

4. Premium design builds trust.

5. Build for players first.

6. Every page should answer a question.

---

## Success

When players have a GTA 6 question, their first instinct should be:

**"Open Atlas."**
