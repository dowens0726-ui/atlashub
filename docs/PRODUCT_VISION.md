# Atlas Product Vision

## Mission

Atlas exists to help gamers spend less time searching and more time playing.

We build intelligent companions that guide players before, during, and after every gaming session.

---

# Vision

Atlas will become the operating system for gaming.

Not just a website.

Not just a game guide.

A personalized gaming companion that helps players know what to do next.

Steam launches games.

Atlas helps you play them.

---

# Our Principles

## Player First

Every feature must improve the player's experience.

If it doesn't save time, reduce frustration, or increase enjoyment, we don't build it.

---

## Build Systems, Not Pages

Everything we create should be reusable.

One great system is worth more than one hundred handcrafted pages.

---

## Trust Before Revenue

We earn trust first.

Revenue is a result of building something players genuinely love.

---

## Quality Over Quantity

Every page should feel like the definitive resource.

Better to build 100 amazing pages than 10,000 average ones.
