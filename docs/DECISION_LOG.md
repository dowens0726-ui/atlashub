# Decision Log

Every important architectural or product decision is recorded here.

---

## 2026

### Atlas will always be player-first.

Reason:
Long-term trust is more valuable than short-term revenue.

---

### AtlasHub is the first product.

Reason:
Atlas is the company.
AtlasHub is the GTA companion.

Future products can share the same platform.

---

### Build systems, not pages.

Reason:
Reusable systems scale.
One-off solutions create technical debt.

---

### The free version must be genuinely useful.

Reason:
Subscriptions should unlock convenience,
not essential information.
