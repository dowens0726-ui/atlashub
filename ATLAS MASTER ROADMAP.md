📅 Year 1 Goal

Become the best free GTA VI companion on the web.

Primary objectives:

Build a complete GTA VI database.
Rank well in search results.
Reach the first 10,000 monthly users.
Launch Atlas Alpha and iterate based on feedback.
Begin earning the first recurring revenue through ads or Atlas Pro.

Success looks like:

People recommend Atlas because it's the fastest way to find GTA VI information.
A small but growing community returns regularly.
📅 Year 2 Goal

Expand Atlas into a gaming companion platform.

Support additional games while keeping GTA VI first-class.

Potential expansions:

Red Dead Redemption
Cyberpunk
Elder Scrolls VI
Minecraft
Other games that fit the companion model

Platform features:

Accounts
Cloud Sync
Favorites across games
Shared search
Cross-game dashboard

Success looks like:

Atlas is known as a companion platform, not just a GTA website.
Multiple active game databases.
A sustainable business with recurring users and revenue.
📅 Year 3 Goal

Become the premier companion platform for open-world games.

Long-term initiatives:

Native iOS App
Native Android App
Atlas Pro subscriptions
Interactive maps for every supported game
Personalized dashboards
AI-powered recommendations
Public API (if it makes sense)
Community features (lists, ratings, guides)

Success looks like:

Atlas has a loyal community.
The platform is financially sustainable.
Development can continue consistently without relying on a single game's launch cycle.

Every feature should answer one question:

Does this help players spend less time searching and more time playing?

Build trust before monetization.
